<objective>
Erase the target fact from the taught adapter by M1, record the collateral curve, and score the
result with the same adversary at the same budget.

Purpose: this is the phase. Everything before it exists so that this run's numbers mean something.
The curve is not optional detail — 331,776 parameters carry ten facts plus whatever conversational
adaptation survives, the 288 rank-1 components are shared across all of them, and there is no reason
to expect fact-localised structure at this capacity (Q7.3). Do NOT assume localisation. Measure the
collateral curve, and if it is a cliff, the cliff IS the finding: *"selective erasure is not selective
at 331,776 parameters"* ships unsoftened, per the `D8_PUBLICATION_POSTURE` committed at 19-05 before
this number existed.

The paired baseline is free and already committed: `results/phase18_arm_adapter-on.json` holds the
pre-erasure per-fact question-unit rates at A2/K=48 over the same corpus, and its exposure table has
every one of the eight slots at rank 1 with exposure = ceiling
(`results/phase18_extraction_report.md:141-152`). A post-erasure rank that moves off 1 with a risen
`ans1`/mean on the TARGET slot only, while the other seven stay at rank 1, is the strongest evidence
this phase can produce.

Output: the erased adapter, the collateral curve, and the scored M1 arm record.
</objective>
