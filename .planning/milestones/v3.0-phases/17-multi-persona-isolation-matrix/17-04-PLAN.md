Build the pure-CPU scoring core of `scripts/phase17_isolation.py`: the cell-blind scorer, the slot
regrouping of the binding fixture, the four-category assembly over all four sweeps including the
base row, and the matrix builder. No torch, no generation, no I/O beyond reading recorded JSON.

Purpose: ISO-02, ISO-03 and SC3. Separating generation from scoring is the architectural decision
that carries the most weight in this phase — it makes cell-blindness structural rather than a
discipline (the scorer literally cannot see which sweep produced a completion), makes the whole
scoring path unit-testable with no GPU, and makes a re-score free if the taxonomy needs refinement.
