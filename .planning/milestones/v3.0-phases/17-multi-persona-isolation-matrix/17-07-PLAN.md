<objective>
Run `scripts/phase17_persona_gate.py` against the frozen un-adapted base and the frozen tokenizer,
and take the blocking human GO/ADAPT verdict on the quoted evidence.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC2 makes the verdict a hard blocker. Its judgment is semantic proximity on quoted
base completions — precisely what exact-match cannot see (Phase 14's own reasoning) — so the
measurement is automated and only the decision is manual. RESEARCH F-13 records what a clean result
buys: the base producing zero containments of each minted value means an off-diagonal hit later
cannot be the base's own prior, it must come from adapter *i*.

Output: `results/phase17_personas_report.md`, committed, with a hand-recorded verdict.
