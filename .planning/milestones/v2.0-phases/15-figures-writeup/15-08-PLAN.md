    def normalize_quote(text: str) -> str:
        # Whitespace and blockquote markers ONLY. See 15-05-PLAN.md <verbatim_normalization>.
        # Strip at most ONE leading '>' (plus at most one following space) per line, then
        # collapse every whitespace run — newlines included — to a single space.
        unquoted = re.sub(r"(?m)^[ \t]*>[ \t]?", "", text)
        return re.sub(r"\s+", " ", unquoted).strip()

no word, digit, punctuation mark, emphasis marker, or the `…` truncation ellipsis — because that
whitespace-only scope is the entire basis of the rule's legitimacy. Adding any further
substitution (case folding, punctuation stripping, ellipsis handling) is a violation of D-15, not
a refinement of the helper.
