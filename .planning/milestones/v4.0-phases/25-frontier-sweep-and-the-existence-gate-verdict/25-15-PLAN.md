<objective>
Run the control at both capacities, fire the reproduction gate on its reading, and measure the n=64
leg's own matched-control floor — the three things every later point's interpretability rests on.

Purpose: if the control's recall does not reproduce 790/1008 at seed 1337, the fact-aligned recipe
changed something and **every later point is uninterpretable**. That is why `prove_reproduction` halts
at zero sweep points rather than logging a warning, and why it runs here rather than at assembly.

One correction travels with this plan and must be written into its records. D-01 justified the ~2.2 h
of extraction scoring per capacity by claiming `mitigation_point_verdict` *requires*
`control_extraction_successes` / `_questions`. Measured: those kwargs feed `extraction_ceiling`, which
proves the provenance arm is the **never-taught** arm — so the gate never reads the control's
extraction at all. The decision stands on independent grounds: under CTRL-02 the control **is** a sweep
point, FRONT-03 demands per-question counts for every point, and the control anchors the
privacy × utility plane. The justification is corrected, not the decision.

Output: two control point records, the n=64 matched floor, and their guard.
</objective>
