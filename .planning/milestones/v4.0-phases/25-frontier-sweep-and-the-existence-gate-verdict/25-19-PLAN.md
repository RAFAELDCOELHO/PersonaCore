Assemble the single artifact this phase exists to produce, prove its ordering and its internal
consistency at the one write that creates it, and draw the figures from it and nothing else.

Purpose: FRONT-03's promise is strong — **counts, never rates; every bound re-derivable; one file**.
That promise is kept by three mechanisms landing at the same instant. The write-once emitter refuses an
existing artifact and refuses a dirty tree across `scripts/`, `src/`, `results/` and `artifacts/`, which
holds only because the driver committed all 44 point records as they landed. The ordered `point_keys`
hard equality is proved **at that single write**, so a missing or duplicated point cannot reach the
artifact. And `held_out_generalization` is asserted to re-derive exactly from the per-point A2 counts
beside it, so an aggregate can never drift from its own data.

The ε reporting lands here in its strongest form: both multiplicities named with the frozen pin's own
`RECORDED, NOT RESOLVED` status carried through, the curve total by basic composition across both legs,
`selection_accounted = False`, and the declaration that publishing the σ=0 control leaves no joint bound
over all published artifacts.

Output: `results/phase25_frontier.json`, the figures, and their guard.
</objective>
