legacy clip structurally unreachable on that path, and prove both halves: seam off is bit-identical
to the Phase-10 golden fixture, and the private noised term is byte-identical with and without the
public replay term.

`align_facts=None` playbook is the established pattern — additive kwarg, default byte-identical,
proven against a golden fixture — and DPSGD-02 demands the proof explicitly rather than by analogy.

clip, the `dp_noise_rng` save refresh, and V-14 / V-12 / D-06's σ=0 identity in
`tests/test_phase22_dpsgd.py`.
