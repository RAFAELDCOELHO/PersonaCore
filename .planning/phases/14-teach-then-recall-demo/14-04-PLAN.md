<objective>
Author the template-family grammar and build the masked teaching bins, with the three mechanical
held-out guarantees (family disjointness, string-level absence, token-level non-subsequence) pinned
by CPU tests.

Purpose: DEMO-06's claim is that the fact was internalized independent of phrasing *structure*
(D-13). That only holds if entire families are held out and nothing from a held-out family leaks into
the bin. The masking regime also reverses Phase 12's verdict — by design, not drift: stage-2 LM
tuning legitimately trains on both speakers, but personalization/QA teaching must cover ANSWER tokens
only (PITFALLS-14), or the model learns to imitate questions instead of answering.

Output: the grammar in `scripts/phase14_factset.py`, the bins half of `scripts/teach_persona.py`, and
`tests/test_phase14_teaching.py`.

<structural_gap_resolutions>
**S2 — `MASK_FRACTION_BAND` does not transfer, and this plan states the Phase-14 band and its derivation.**

`scripts/prepare_dialog_corpus.py:45` pins `(0.30, 0.70)` for PersonaChat episodes, where multi-turn
dialogue splits roughly evenly between the two speakers. A Phase-14 teaching episode is
`<|system|><|user|>{question}<|assistant|>{answer}<eos>` with mask=1 on answer content plus the
terminating eos ONLY. From the measured F5 ranges — 26–45 ids per episode, 11–24 answer-content ids —
the realizable fraction spans `(11+1)/45 = 0.267` (short answer, long question) to `(24+1)/26 = 0.96`
(long answer, minimal question). **PersonaChat's 0.70 ceiling sits inside the legitimate Phase-14
range**, so copying the literal is a false-failure waiting to happen on an answer-heavy QA corpus —
which is exactly what this corpus is by construction.

**Phase-14 value: `MASK_FRACTION_BAND = (0.15, 0.95)`.** Its only job is to catch the two mechanical
mask bugs, and both produce degenerate values: a mask that is never set gives exactly 0.0, and a mask
applied to everything gives exactly 1.0. Every intermediate value is legitimate for this corpus
shape. Span-level correctness — the actual Pitfall-14 off-by-one bug family — is pinned separately and
much more sharply by `test_answer_span_mask`'s hand-written literal fixture, which is where that
guarantee belongs.

Because a wide guard proves little on its own, the **measured** fraction is not hidden behind it: the
builder prints `mask fraction: mean/min/max` per arm and records the number in the run provenance
echo, and plan 14-09 carries it into `results/phase14_calibration_report.md`. The real number becomes
committed evidence; the band stays a sanity guard.
</structural_gap_resolutions>
