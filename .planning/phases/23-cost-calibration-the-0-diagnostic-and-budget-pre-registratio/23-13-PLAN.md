<objective>
Commit Z — the sweep width, the per-point draw budget K and the step budget — set **from** the
measurements CAL-01 and CAL-05 just produced, in a module the gate cannot reach.

Purpose: CAL-02 requires the resource budget to be separated from the outcome gate, structurally, so
a reader cannot mistake a resource calibration for an outcome-threshold peek. The separation is
already enforced two ways (statically since Phase 20, transitively since 23-02). What lands here is
the content: numbers that did not exist until 23-11 measured them, pinned as literals that re-derive
from the committed cost record on every suite run.

D-06 also resolves here. `results/phase23_cal03_wiring.json` carries the verdict on whether N leaks
into T; if it came back falsified, the n=64 leg is not committed and the withdrawing measurement is
recorded. The n=8 leg stays intact and publishable either way — a data-path wiring bug does not
indict the DP mechanism, which is the scope distinction separating this from D-04's halt.

Output: `scripts/mitigation_budget.py` carrying Z with `_PROVENANCE` siblings, and the tests that
make every constant checkable.
</objective>
