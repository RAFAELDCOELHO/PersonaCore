<objective>
Run the ONE scheduling this phase's pre-registration depends on: the unmitigated control at N seeds
and the never-taught arm at the same N seeds — and commit the measured noise floor **before the σ=0
run exists**.

Purpose: D-03 makes the floor a measurement rather than an assumption, and makes the ordering
structural. D-08 folds CTRL-03's never-taught arm into the same scheduling, which converts a known
Phase-25 refusal (the FROZEN gate demands ≥ 2 distinct seeds for the extraction floor and CTRL-03's
text says "trained once") into a decision resolved here, at ~20 s of training per arm.

Open Question 4 — how many seeds, 3, 4 or 5 — is answered by costing the **scoring** leg first,
because training does not bind: the non-DP arm is ~20.4 s while a full-fidelity scoring pass is
hours. The rule that turns that measurement into a choice of N is written down **before** the
measurement is taken.

Output: `scripts/phase23_run.py`, `results/phase23_control_floor.json` and
`results/phase23_never_taught_training.json`, both committed, with the control record's first add
strictly preceding anything σ=0 writes.
</objective>

| measurement → pinned constant | The floor becomes a pre-registered number that D-04 halts a whole sweep on. If it can move after σ=0 is seen, the pre-registration is theatre. |
