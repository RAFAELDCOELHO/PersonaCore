<objective>
Run the protocol-matched comparator at five seeds and re-reduce the noise floor over its readings.

**What is being replaced and why.** `scripts/mitigation_budget.py`'s `CONTROL_NOISE_FLOOR =
0.05357142857142849` was reduced over the OLD control protocol — 8 random windows per step drawn from
a bin that is 51.94% replay, a teaching loss weight of `p = 2719/6262 = 0.4342`, and `grad_clip = 1.0`
binding on 19 of 25 steps at mean shrink `0.8071`. That is not the protocol the σ=0 arm ran, so the
floor describes the wrong comparator. The corrected comparator equalises all three mechanisms
together; this plan measures what it actually reads.

**Wall clock, sized from committed measurements rather than estimated.** Per seed the training leg is
bounded ABOVE by the σ=0 arm's own measured `seconds_total = 205.44225783273578`
(`results/phase23_sigma_zero.json` → `training`), because the comparator draws the identical
65-window lot in the identical 12 micro-batches while dropping `absorb_record`'s per-record clip +
sum + drain over 72 LoRA tensors (1,600 calls per run) and `finalize`; it adds 200 `clip_grad_norm_`
calls and 1,600 `/accum` divides, both negligible against that. The bound is conservative for a
second measured reason: that `205.44` bracket covers *"the whole `train_arm` call … + both
end-of-run `masked_perplexity` sweeps"* (the record's own `bracket_covers` field), and the
comparator runs NEITHER sweep. The scoring leg is the identical instrument and measured
`983.21`-`1026.87` s across the five old control seeds (`results/phase23_control_floor.json` →
`scoring_seconds_per_seed`), plus `952.07` s on the σ=0 arm.
**So per seed ≤ 205.44 + 1026.87 = 1,232.31 s, and five seeds ≤ 6,161.6 s ≈ 103 minutes** on MPS.
For scale: 23-08's scheduling took ~2h50m and 23-10's σ=0 leg ~95 min end to end.

Purpose: produce a floor that describes the comparator the σ=0 arm will actually be judged against.
Output: `results/phase23_matched_control.json` plus five run CSVs, committed.
</objective>
