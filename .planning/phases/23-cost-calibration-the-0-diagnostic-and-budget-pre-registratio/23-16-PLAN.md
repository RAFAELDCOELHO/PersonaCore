**The obstacle the planning brief flagged, and why it dissolves.** The brief warns that "a non-DP arm
cannot reach the `dp_kwargs` seam today — `build_arm_bins` reads `DP_ARMS` and nothing else, and
widening `DP_ARMS` would force the arm to carry a sigma." That is true of `build_arm_bins` and FALSE
of `train()`. Read `src/personacore/training/loop.py:508-548`: the fact-aligned seam's three refusals
are gated on `_fact = {"fact_bin": fact_bin, "n_facts": n_facts}` — *"Gated on the FACT half only"* —
and `loop.py:683` gates the replay pass on `replay_windows is not None`. Neither mentions `dp_fn`.
So the seam change the brief asks to be planned is **no seam change at all**: the comparator calls
`tp.train(...)` directly with the six data kwargs and `dp_fn` absent, exactly as
`phase23_run.train_never_taught` already calls `tp.train(...)` directly for an arm
`build_arm_bins` cannot express. `DP_ARMS` is not widened; `teach_persona.py` is not edited.

What remains genuinely new is small and all of it is in `scripts/phase23_run.py`: one kwargs
builder, one capture bracket for `clip_grad_norm_`, one training leg, and a preflight that runs
23-15's three AST gates.

Purpose: make the comparator exist as code whose protocol match is checked by construction, with
every refusal reachable at zero GPU cost.
