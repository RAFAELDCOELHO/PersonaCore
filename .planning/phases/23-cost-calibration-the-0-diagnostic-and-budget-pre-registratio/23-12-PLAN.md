chosen without it."* Measured at the production shape on MPS, that reproduces for the **non-DP** arm

locked decision changes; what changes is the margin, and a plan budgeting `16 × 17 s` of training for
an n=64 sweep is short by roughly eight hours.

active"*, so measuring it and finding a different number is the requirement being satisfied rather
than a defect being found. 23-11 took that measurement; this plan lands the correction in the same
phase, so the retraction and the evidence supporting it arrive together.

Output: dated additive continuations in all three files carrying the claim, and a structural test
that the correction is additive and complete.
