<objective>
Measure the two costs that size the sweep: the DP training leg at the expensive capacity, and the
generation throughput on a genuinely noised adapter — bracketed floor to ceiling rather than reported
as a mean.

Purpose: the roadmap prices a 16-point n=64 sweep's training leg at `16 × 17 s`. Measured on MPS at
the production shape, `dp_n64` is ≈ **30 minutes per arm**, so that budget is short by roughly **8
hours**. In the other direction, the committed 4.77 h/point generation figure was measured on the
un-adapted base where 45–56 of 64 draws terminated on a stop id; forcing every draw to run the full
48 tokens costs **1.536×** wall clock, putting a noised point's ceiling at **7.33 h**. Both of these
are pre-sizing brackets from research; CAL-01 and CAL-05 require the real measurements, and this plan
takes them.

Both live in one plan because one `dp_n64` run at σ > 0 produces both: the training wall clock CAL-01
asks for, and the noised adapter CAL-05 must re-measure throughput on.

Output: `results/phase23_cost.json`, and the noised run's own record under
`phase23_prereg.noised_record_path(...)`.
</objective>
