Add `adversarial_ratio` to `build_bins` as a build-time mixture ratio, interleaved by a seed-derived
permutation, byte-identical at its default — and prove the kwarg is actually read before claiming
the byte-identity means anything.

Purpose: ADVT-01's whole claim is "no new training seam". `replay_ratio` already established the
shape: a mixture baked into the bin rather than into the loop, so `train()` stays untouched and the
ratio is an auditable committed number. The dangerous half is the guard: a byte-identity assertion
over a kwarg nobody reads is trivially true. `test_align_facts_is_wired` exists in this repo for
exactly that reason, and this plan copies it.

Output: the seam, the two new arm rows, and the test module that watched it go from red to green.
