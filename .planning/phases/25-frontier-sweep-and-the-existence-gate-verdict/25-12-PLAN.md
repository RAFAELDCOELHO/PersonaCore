<objective>
Probe the high anchor, then pin the σ ladder, its ε ladder and the two clip constants — in that order,
because D-18 requires the ladder to be committed only after the probe confirms the anchor.

Purpose: this is the phase's only remaining outcome-shaping choice, and every part of it is forced.
The same ladder must serve both capacities, because `capacity_comparison` compares σ under **exact
equality** and reusing one set satisfies `MECHANISM_KEYS` trivially rather than by coincidence. It must
be literals, because `mitigation_budget.py`'s guard runs `ast.literal_eval` on every assigned value, so
a `sigma_for(...)` call cannot be pinned there. And the ε ladder must be pinned at the **full precision
`epsilon_for` returns** rather than at round numbers, because `sigma_for` is a numerical inverse whose
round trip is exact to about one ULP and **not** exact — measured, three ways.

Adding these constants is a three-file change with a **natural RED**: the budget register's hard
equality on the discovered-constants tuple fires the moment they land. It is watched first, then
closed, and the covering test must use **attribute access** because the exclusion walk collects
`ast.Attribute.attr` only.

Output: the probe record, four new pins with provenance, the register entries, `tests/test_phase25_grid.py`,
and WR-05's additive multiplicity correction.
</objective>
