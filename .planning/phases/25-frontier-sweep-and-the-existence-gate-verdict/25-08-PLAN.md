<objective>
Build the per-point record — the unit that is simultaneously D-10's one-attempt evidence, D-09's
resume boundary and D-31's assembly input — and make a mechanism divergence stop the entire sweep at
the moment the bytes would be written.

Purpose: D-34 is the highest-severity guard in this phase. A point whose live mechanism has drifted
from the pin carries an ε that **does not describe what happened**, and 44 of those assembled into a
published frontier is the failure mode v4.0's research named as the worst available. The guard is
cheap — an exact `==` on five integers and floats — and it must fire at **write** time, before the
bytes exist, because after the write the record is evidence.

Four of the five fields already exist in the Phase 23 point-record schema. `q` is the one genuinely
new field, and its source is `mitigation_unit.SAMPLING_RATE_Q = 1.0` — honest rather than assumed
because under fact-aligned accumulation the private lot **is** the fact set.

Output: `scripts/phase25_record.py` and `tests/test_phase25_record.py`, with all five halts watched.
</objective>
