<objective>
Build the six condition-(c) inputs `mitigation_point_verdict` requires and that **nothing in the
20-plan set produced**, and pre-register — before any sweep point runs — that the retention leg binds
at the anchor.

Purpose: `mitigation_point_verdict` takes 21 kwargs. Measured across all 20 committed plans, **seven
had zero producers and six of them are condition (c)**: `point_dialogue_ppl_on`,
`point_dialogue_ppl_off`, `control_gap`, `gap_noise_floor`, `point_retention_ppl`,
`retention_noise_floor`. No Phase 23 point record carries any of them, so the verdict was
uncomputable. D-35's closing *"Nothing to fix"* rested on that false premise; its ε-scoping claim is
unchanged and still correct.

The cost was measured before the decision rather than estimated after it: **87.4 s per point**, 1.07 h
over 44 (0.80 h with the OFF leg measured once), **0.7-1.0% of the 107-150 h budget**. So cost cannot
argue for a subset, and a subset chosen after seeing which points clear (a) and (b) is the exact
reduction-after-the-fact this milestone forbids everywhere else.

Two things arrive free with it. The measurement path reproduces `results/phase19_arm_erased.json`
**exactly, denominators included**, giving condition (c) a bit-level reproduction check of D-01's
shape. And the anchor arithmetic — already sitting in a committed Phase 19 record and surfaced by no
plan — shows the retention leg **already fails at the untouched taught adapter**. That is the
instrument working: the squeeze between (b)'s recall floor and (c)'s retention cap **is** the
frontier. It is committed in advance so a narrow or empty frontier can never be re-argued afterwards
as a mis-set floor.

Output: `scripts/phase25_condition_c.py` and `tests/test_phase25_condition_c.py`.
</objective>
