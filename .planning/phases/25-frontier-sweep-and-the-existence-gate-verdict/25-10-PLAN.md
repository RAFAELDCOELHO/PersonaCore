<objective>
Build the driver that spends the phase's 100–150 hours, and make its two most dangerous properties
structural rather than declared: the block write is atomic, and its git surface is provably exactly
`{add, commit}` over one path.

Purpose: two facts drive this plan. First, the resume mechanism D-09 asks for **already exists in
full** in `scripts/phase23_run.py` — the identity-refusing load, the skip-complete-shape branch and
the per-shape write are all there. The single real gap is that the writer is a bare `path.write_text`
over ~970 KB with no atomic helper anywhere in the repository, so a kill mid-write destroys the whole
point's draws rather than one shape. That is **[BLOCKING]** before any point runs.

Second, the user resolved the one architectural tension this phase carried. `.planning/STATE.md`
records the driver's git surface as read-only and the commit as the operator's act at the process
boundary — which cannot hold across an unattended 6-day LaunchAgent run with `KeepAlive` false and 44
commits owed. **The driver commits, strictly scoped to the record of the point it just completed, and
that scoping is verified by real inspection of the driver's code.** Prose saying "narrowly scoped" is
not the guarantee; an AST walk is.

Output: `scripts/phase25_run.py` and `tests/test_phase25_driver.py`.
</objective>
