<objective>
Build the plotter and retarget Phase 15's figure guard onto it — porting the three parts that transfer
and authoring the one clause that does not exist yet.

Purpose: FRONT-03 promises the frontier artifact is the **single source of truth** and that every
figure is drawn only from it. That promise is worth exactly what its guard is worth. Phase 15 already
converted "the plotter never opens a checkpoint" from a declared invariant into a checked mechanism —
PROJECT.md records it as one of three such conversions — and Phase 23 applied the mirrored form to the
budget-import guard. This plan retargets that machinery, and adds the piece Phase 15 never needed: an
**allow-list**, so the plotter may open the frontier artifact and nothing else.

The plotter is built now, before any point exists, precisely because the guard is a millisecond CPU
check and discovering a figure is not regenerable after 150 hours of compute is the failure this
phase's whole validation contract exists to prevent.

Output: `scripts/plot_phase25.py` and `tests/test_phase25_plots.py`, with four watched REDs.
</objective>

|----------|-------------|
| the committed artifact → every published figure | FRONT-03 promises figures are drawn only from it; a second read makes the promise unverifiable |

| a path constant in two modules → a rename | two spellings of the artifact path drift silently unless asserted equal |
