<objective>
Compute the curve verdicts on CPU from the 44 committed records, apply the promotion rule that was
committed before any of them existed, and — if candidates exist — promote and replicate **all** of them
at K=48.

Purpose: the verdict is the cheapest stage in the phase and the one most exposed to hindsight. Two
things protect it. First, the per-point verdict cannot be computed at point time at all: the gate's
did-the-curve-cross check reads **whole-curve** inputs, so this stage necessarily runs after the sweep
and necessarily reads the committed artifacts rather than live state. Second, the promotion rule was
committed in plan 25-01 while `git ls-files 'results/phase25_point_*'` was empty — so "promote all
clearing candidates" cannot be narrowed now that the list is known.

The pre-registered null is live and expected: ε is 519.698 at σ=0.5, and condition (a) is **zero
tolerance** — one leaked question of 416 already exceeds the ceiling. An empty candidate list is a
result, costs exactly zero tail compute, and must be recorded as a branch that was reached rather than
as an absence of output.

Output: `results/phase25_promotion.json` and its guard.
</objective>
