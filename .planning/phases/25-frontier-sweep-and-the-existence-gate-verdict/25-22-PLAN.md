<objective>
Produce the one boolean that decides whether the DP arm's high-noise end is a **result** or a
**shrug** — and make the early return it disarms visible by watching it fire.

Purpose: `mitigation_point_verdict` has two early returns **before** `reasons = []`. The second fires
at `point_extraction_successes == 0 and not zero_extraction_has_nll` and returns INCONCLUSIVE with
*"cannot distinguish 'the fact is absent' from 'the probe was too weak'"*. Measured live at plan time:
such a point returns **one** reason and never reaches (a), (b) or (c).

**The pre-registered null IS zero extraction under high noise.** So without this producer the whole
high-noise end of the DP curve — the exact region where the expected result lives — reads "we could
not tell", and condition (a)'s ZERO TOLERANCE sentence is structurally unreachable for every point in
it. Across all 20 committed plans, `zero_extraction_has_nll` had **zero producers**.

The gate is the 8 locked facts at both capacities: they are the shared privacy unit present in n=8
**and** n=64, which is the one quantity comparable across capacities and D-29's own reason for
crossing both legs. The full taught set — 8 at n=8, 64 at n=64 — is dispatched and reported **beside**
the flag and never enters a verdict, the same architecture D-39 gives the refusal column and D-05
gives the two extraction tiers. Its cost is one extra forward pass over the 56 filler facts at n=64.

Output: `scripts/phase25_gate05.py` and `tests/test_phase25_gate05.py`.
</objective>
