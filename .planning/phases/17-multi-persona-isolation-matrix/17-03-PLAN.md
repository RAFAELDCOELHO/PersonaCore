<objective>
Mint the 24 persona values — 3 personas x 8 core slots, contradictory in every slot — as committed
`Fact` literals in `scripts/phase17_personas.py`, screened through the four mechanical filters
committed in 17-01, with the measured token census transcribed as a literal dict.

the single most likely way this phase produces a worthless green result. D-04 requires collision in
all eight core slots with no non-colliding subset; D-06 requires all 24 minted fresh, because
`GATE_REJECTED_CANDIDATES` is also the contradiction-detector lexicon and reusing it would stop the
detector separating "leaked j's value" from "answered its own".

Output: the committed persona material, its census, and the CPU tests that prove all four filters
bite on it. The GPU guessability half is 17-05/17-07 — it is checkpoint-specific and structurally
cannot be a CPU test.
