<objective>
Replicate the worst-colliding pair across k=3 seeds and report it descriptively, so seed variance is
not mistaken for interference.

Purpose: ISO-05. The selection rule and the seeds were both committed in plan 17-01, before the
matrix existed; the mode that renders them was committed in plan 17-11, before any replication number
existed. Selecting after the fact would be legitimate only because the output is descriptive, but a
committed rule costs nothing and removes the argument. D-19 fixes k=3 as the original seed plus two
more per persona of the selected pair: 4 extra adapters and 4 extra sweeps, ~23 min.

**This plan writes no code.** Every artifact it produces comes out of `--train`, `--sweep` and
`--replicate`, all committed in Waves 3-5. That is the point: the whole pipeline was in git history
before it produced a number, and an ad-hoc script written here to render a public artifact after the
numbers exist would be the one place in this phase where that rule was broken.

Output: per-seed off-diagonal rates for the selected pair, appended to the isolation report as an
addendum with zero deletions.
</objective>

`results/phase17_isolation_report.md`.
