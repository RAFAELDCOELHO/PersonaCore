<objective>
Build the pure-CPU scoring core of `scripts/phase17_isolation.py`: the cell-blind scorer, the slot
regrouping of the binding fixture, the four-category assembly, and the matrix builder. No torch, no
generation, no I/O beyond reading recorded JSON.

Purpose: ISO-02 and SC3. Separating generation from scoring is the architectural decision that
carries the most weight in this phase — it makes cell-blindness structural rather than a discipline
(the scorer literally cannot see which sweep produced a completion), makes the whole scoring path
unit-testable with no GPU, and makes a re-score free if the taxonomy needs refinement.

Output: the scoring core, its tests, and the empirical confirmation of what shape a silently failed
adapter swap actually produces — which ROADMAP SC1's second 2026-08-14 amendment records as MEDIUM
confidence and explicitly defers to this implementation.
</objective>
