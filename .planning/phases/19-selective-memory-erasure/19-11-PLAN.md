<objective>
Lock the three measured constants — the `921a6bc` analogue — in their own file, guarded to precede
every target artifact, and prove reachability once more against the number that actually landed.

Purpose: §Q6's two-file split. The (a) floor constant CANNOT live in the pinned file, because it
cannot exist until a `results/phase19_*` artifact does, and writing it there would turn the ancestry
guard permanently red (P19-3). Phase 17 established the split for exactly this reason and Phase 18
refused it for the opposite one; Phase 19 has a sanctioned post-artifact write, so it needs Phase
17's shape (`tests/test_phase16_prereg.py:162-171`, `STATE.md:194`).

The reachability re-check is not a formality. 19-03 proved the RULE could not produce an unclearable
floor for any `cal_rate`. This proves the FLOOR that landed is clearable, against the same named
attainable outcome — 0 successes over 27 questions. If the two ever disagree, one of them is wrong
and it must be found here, before the target is touched.

Output: `scripts/phase19_floor.py`, its ancestry guard, and a human's confirmation that the floor and
its branch are what the pinned rule produces.
</objective>
