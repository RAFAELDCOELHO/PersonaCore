strictly toward the harder side, at the same four decimals Phase 14 published. Record in
`ERASURE_FLOOR_RULE` that the deviation from Phase 14's literal `round` is deliberate and is the same
mirror as the clamp — Phase 14's `round` sat against a `>=` gate where rounding up was the harder
direction; against a `<=` cap it is the easier one.

**A single fact's rate, declared as a departure.** Phase 14's `lock_thresholds` consumes the

erasure.
