    confound importance with tensor size — fc_in/fc_out have 4x the elements of the attention
    projections. Record `fisher_aggregate: "mean"` at the top level so the choice is in the data.

  copied from `continual/fisher.py`. Ratios are `float(torch.linalg.norm(dw) /
