## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| adapter-on arm → adapter-off arm | Any difference other than the adapter gate invalidates the contrast; the defence is one corpus, one loader, two processes |
| harness → every claim downstream | The positive control is what makes "we found nothing" falsifiable; a broken harness must stop the phase, not qualify it |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-18-15-01 | Tampering | Something changes between the arms — corpus, K, seeds, mask | mitigate | Five-field pairing verification on the recorded artifacts; `git diff --exit-code` on the driver and corpus between the runs |
| T-18-15-02 | Tampering | A rerun overwrites recorded completions after a disappointing arm | mitigate | Clobber refusal with no force flag; the honest recovery is a reviewed deletion commit |
| T-18-15-03 | Repudiation | A broken harness is qualified instead of declared broken | mitigate | Blocking human checkpoint with an explicit STOP branch; no tolerance parameter exists to reach for |
| T-18-15-04 | Repudiation | A zero recorded without its NLL and exposure, leaving it uninterpretable | mitigate | Acceptance asserts every zero target carries a finite NLL and an exposure rank; `null_result_is_admissible` forces INCONCLUSIVE otherwise |
| T-18-15-05 | Spoofing | The off arm built from a separate un-adapted model rather than the gated one | mitigate | `run_arm` uses one loader and `adapter_disabled`; asserted structurally in 18-10 |
| T-18-15-SC | Tampering | package installs | accept | Zero installs; `pyproject.toml` sha256-pinned |
