## Why exactly one tier can be gated (D-07)

D-10 forbids pooling taught with held-out, so each fact yields two numbers. Gating both takes the
Holm family from 6 to 12, `alpha` drops to `0.05/12 = 0.0041667`, and 8/8 unanimity

unanimity in both tiers.

## Why ties count AGAINST (D-08)

Never inflates significance, and the denominator is locked at pre-registration time (STAT-02).
Discarding ties — the textbook rule — is rejected on two grounds: one tie gives `n = 7` where
unanimity is `p = 0.015625 > 0.0083333` (unclearable); and decisively, the prompt-stuffed x
base-neither pair is expected to tie on nearly all 8 facts given Phase 14's committed `1/1944`,
which would drop that pair to `n ≈ 0` where the test is **undefined**, not merely unclearable, and
the Holm family becomes ill-formed. Under "ties against", that pair simply becomes 0/8.

## The direction filter — D-29, and the defect it closes (READ THIS BEFORE WRITING THE TEST)

D-08 originally claimed 0/8 gives `p = 1.0`. **That was wrong under a pure two-sided test**, where
0/8 is exactly as extreme as 8/8 — both give `0.0078125`. As written, a pair on which *all eight
facts tied* would have entered the Holm family as a SIGNIFICANT result: a false positive in the
over-claiming direction, which is the failure mode this milestone exists to prevent. CONTEXT.md
carries the dated correction; D-29 is the resolution.

**D-29: the sign test is two-sided in MAGNITUDE and directional in ALTERNATIVE.**
`sign_test_exact` returns the two-sided p when `positives > SIGN_TEST_N / 2`, and **`1.0`

of the 6 `HOLM_FAMILY_PAIRS` entries **before any run** — without it the direction could be fixed
after seeing the signs, which is precisely what STAT-05 forbids, in the one phase whose entire
product is a pre-registration.
