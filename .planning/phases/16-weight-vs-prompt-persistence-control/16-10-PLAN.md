## PERSONA_CAP does NOT constrain this path — the inherited premise was wrong

An earlier draft of this plan asserted that `PERSONA_CAP = 140` caps the persona span, so dilution
past 140 tokens must come from added turns. **That is false on this route**, and the false premise
is recorded here so Phase 17/18 do not re-inherit it:

- `run_fairness_control` -> `build_recall_prompt` -> `encode_dialogue(tok, persona, [(question, "")])`
  admits **exactly one turn**. There is no turns axis to dilute along.
- `PERSONA_CAP` is enforced **only** by `cap_persona` (`src/personacore/dialogue/serialize.py:115`),
  and `build_recall_prompt` (`:92`) **never calls it**. The cap does not bite on this path.

Consequence: the persona span reaches 448 tokens directly, and **all dilution happens inside the
persona span**. Do not add a multi-turn builder, do not touch `serialize.py`, and do not add files
to `files_modified` beyond what the sweep needs.

**This correction is not plan-local.** ROADMAP SC5 and REQUIREMENTS PERS-03 both said "dilution
across turns" and were amended to **"dilution within the persona span"** at `79fa01a`, with dated
notes; RESEARCH Pitfall 4's `[VERIFIED]`-stamped sentence was struck and corrected in the same pass.
PERS-03 is THIS plan's requirement, so the amended wording is the one the report must evidence — a
report showing persona-span-internal dilution satisfies SC5, and there is no turns axis for it to be
missing.

## Sweep wall-clock budget — record it so a slow cell is not read as a hang

7 cells (6 dilution + 1 overwrite) x 270 questions x ~3.18 s median **≈ 100 min**, and that is the
FLOOR. The 3.18 s median was measured at the **46-token** nominal prompt; there is no KV cache
(D-04), so every decode step recomputes the full forward and per-question cost grows with prompt
length. The 320- and 448-token cells will run materially slower than nominal. **Budget 100 min to
~3 h for the sweep. A single cell taking 2 h is EXPECTED, not a hang — do not kill it and do not
shrink the cell set to make the clock fit.**
