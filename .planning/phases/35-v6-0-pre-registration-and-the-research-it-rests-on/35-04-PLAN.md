A slot is still filled exactly once, anywhere. Plan 05 shows this reading to Rafael. The (a) leg checks EVERY commit of a fill file, not only its first one: that is the `_assert_frozen_before` precedent (a post-record edit of a fill file is a post-hoc fill) and it implies the first-commit condition. The (c) leg exempts the declared inputs of EVERY slot of the owner, not only the unfilled slot's own (B6): Phase 41's two later slots consume two different in-phase records, and (b) forbids an input in the same commit as its fill, so a per-slot exemption reddens every legal Phase 41 sequence at its intermediate commits.
